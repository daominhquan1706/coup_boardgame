Privacy Policy

App: Coup Boardgame
Effective date: April 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Coup Boardgame ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Coup Boardgame mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous authentication identifier created by Firebase Authentication (for example, a user ID/UID).
  • Player profile data that you provide in-app, such as player name and display name.
  • Gameplay and room data needed to run multiplayer matches, such as room code, player state, actions, game events, and timestamps.
  • Local on-device data for app functionality (for example saved player/display name and preferences).

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain game sessions and player state.
  • To identify players in rooms and during gameplay.
  • To provide game history, game logic, and synchronization.
  • To maintain app security, stability, and operations.

3. How Information Is Shared

  • With other players in the same room: your display name and gameplay actions are visible to participants in that room.
  • With service providers that process data for app operation, such as: Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google).
  • If required by law or legal process, or to protect rights and safety.

4. Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to operate and improve the app, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Data stored locally on your device can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

5. Children’s Privacy

Coup Boardgame is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of your data. You can also stop using the app at any time and uninstall it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email listed on the Google Play listing for Coup Boardgame.
